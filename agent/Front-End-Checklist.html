- **Description**: The checklist includes performance optimization tips such as minimizing HTTP requests, optimizing images, and leveraging browser caching.
- **Business Value**: Faster load times improve user experience and lead to higher conversion rates. For businesses, a performance-optimized website can result in increased sales, lower bounce rates, and better user retention.

- **Description**: The checklist covers web accessibility standards (WCAG), ensuring that websites are usable for individuals with disabilities.

- **Description**: The checklist includes best practices for SEO, such as using proper tagging, structured data, and meta descriptions.
- **Business Value**: Optimizing for search engines helps businesses increase their visibility online, potentially driving more organic traffic to their sites. Enhanced visibility directly correlates with lead generation and increased revenue.
