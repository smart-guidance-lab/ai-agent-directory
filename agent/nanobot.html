## 2. Three Specific High-Value Business Use Cases

### 1. Targeted Drug Delivery
Nanobots can be engineered to deliver drugs directly to affected cells, minimizing side effects and increasing treatment efficacy. This personalized medicine approach can lead to improved patient outcomes and reduced healthcare costs.

### 2. Environmental Monitoring and Cleanup
Nanobots can be deployed for environmental applications, such as detecting pollutants in water and soil, and even breaking down hazardous materials at a molecular level, leading to sustainable environmental management practices.

### 3. Precision Manufacturing
In manufacturing, nanobots can assemble materials at a molecular level, allowing for the creation of products with enhanced properties (e.g., strength, conductivity). This can lead to significant reductions in costs and materials waste while improving product performance.
