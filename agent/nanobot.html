### Business Use Cases

1. **Medical Applications**
**Description:** Nanobots can be employed in targeted drug delivery systems, allowing for precise treatment of diseases at the cellular level. This minimizes side effects and enhances the efficacy of therapies.
**Value:** Companies in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology fields can leverage this technology to improve patient outcomes and potentially reduce healthcare costs.

2. **Manufacturing and Assembly**
**Description:** In the manufacturing sector, nanobots can be utilized for precision assembly of microcomponents in electronics or for the creation of advanced materials with unique properties at the nanoscale.
**Value:** This can greatly enhance production efficiency, reduce waste, and allow for the creation of innovative products that stand out in the competitive market.

3. **Environmental Monitoring and Remediation**
**Description:** Nanobots can be deployed for environmental cleanup, such as targeting and neutralizing pollutants or contaminants in soil and water sources.
**Value:** Companies focused on environmental sustainability can take advantage of this technology to improve compliance with regulations and enhance their corporate social responsibility initiatives.
