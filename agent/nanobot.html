2. **Environmental Remediation:**
- **Use Case:** Nanobots can be designed to clean up hazardous waste or pollutants in the environment, such as oil spills or toxic heavy metals. By breaking down pollutants at a molecular level, they offer a highly efficient solution to environmental issues.

3. **Manufacturing and Material Enhancement:**
- **Use Case:** In manufacturing, nanobots can manipulate materials at the atomic level to create stronger, lighter, and more efficient products. Applications include electronics, automotive, and aerospace industries where performance improvements can lead to significant cost savings.
- **Business Value:** Companies that adopt nanobot technology in manufacturing can enhance product quality, reduce material waste, and gain a competitive edge in innovation and production efficiency.
