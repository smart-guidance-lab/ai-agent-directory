## 2. High-Value Business Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Medical Treatment and Drug Delivery
- **Description**: Nanobots can be engineered to deliver drugs directly to affected cells, minimizing side effects and maximizing treatment efficacy.
- **Example**: Targeted cancer therapies, where nanobots recognize and destroy cancer cells while leaving healthy cells unharmed.

### Use Case 2: Environmental Remediation
- **Description**: Nanobots can be deployed to clean up pollutants, filter contaminants from water, or neutralize hazardous materials in an effective and efficient manner.
- **Example**: Utilizing nanobots to remove heavy metals from contaminated water sources, thus restoring safe drinking water.

### Use Case 3: Precision Manufacturing
- **Description**: In manufacturing, nanobots can assemble materials at the molecular level, improving the precision and minimizing waste in production processes.
- **Example**: The creation of nanoscale electronic components that are faster and consume less energy, enhancing the performance of electronic devices.
