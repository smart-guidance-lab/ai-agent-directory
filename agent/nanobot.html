## 2. Three High-Value Business Use Cases

### 2.1 Medical Applications
**Use Case:** Targeted drug delivery systems.

**Description:** Nanobots can be programmed to transport medications directly to diseased cells or tissues, minimizing side effects and increasing treatment efficacy. This precision medicine approach can potentially revolutionize cancer therapy and chronic disease management.

### 2.2 Environmental Remediation
**Use Case:** Pollution cleanup.

**Description:** Nanobots can be deployed to detect and neutralize pollutants at a molecular level. Their ability to break down contaminants in water bodies or soil can be highly beneficial for environmental protection and sustainability efforts.

### 2.3 Manufacturing and Automation
**Use Case:** Advanced manufacturing processes.

**Description:** In manufacturing, nanobots can assemble products at a nanoscale with unparalleled precision. This could lead to a significant reduction in material waste and energy consumption, thereby enhancing production efficiency across various industries.
